## Part B: new findings introduced by the revision

### B-1. Coverage gap (serious) — B10's right-hand side `IRV_spec(decrypt_all(ballots, enclave_privkey, candidates))` is arity-mismatched

- **Category**: Coverage gap (under-specification of correctness obligation)
- **Severity**: serious
- **Scenario**: B10 (line 308): `next.tally_result.tally_body = IRV_spec(decrypt_all(ballots, enclave_privkey, candidates))`. Section 2.5 line 132: `IRV_spec(ballots, candidates) → TallyResult` — takes **two** arguments and produces a `TallyResult`. B10's invocation `IRV_spec(decrypt_all(...))` is **one** argument — the output of `decrypt_all`. Either (a) `decrypt_all` returns a tuple `(ballots, candidates)` and the call is `IRV_spec(decrypt_all(...))` via tuple-spreading (un-stated convention); (b) the syntax is sloppy and should be `IRV_spec(decrypt_all(ballots, enclave_privkey), candidates)`; (c) `IRV_spec` was tacitly extended to take the chain-state quadruple. The doc is silent.

  Worse: Section 2.5's `IRV_spec(ballots, candidates) → TallyResult` produces a `TallyResult` whose schema (line 110-118) has 7 fields: `winners, per_round_counts, eliminated_by_round, ballots_tallied, ballots_dropped, dropped_voters, non_voters`. The IRV algorithm definition (lines 132-142) only describes computing `winners` + `per_round_counts` + `eliminated_by_round`. It says nothing about how `dropped_voters` and `non_voters` are populated by `IRV_spec`. Block E1 step 1 (line 259) handles `dropped_voters` separately *before* calling IRV_spec. So the `IRV_spec` referenced by B10 cannot, on the stated definition, populate the full TallyResult. B10's equality `tally_body = IRV_spec(...)` is type-incomplete.
- **Why it succeeds**: B10 is the load-bearing tally-correctness obligation. A Lean encoder downstream will need to either (i) extend `IRV_spec`'s signature to capture the decrypt/parse/permutation-check phase and the populate-dropped-voters step, or (ii) split B10 into two halves: `dropped_voters / non_voters` computed by a `decrypt_and_validate` function, and `(winners, per_round_counts, eliminated_by_round)` computed by `IRV_spec` on the validated ballot set. The intent doc gives no signal which.
- **Suggested defense**: Either (a) restate `IRV_spec` in Section 2.5 to take raw ciphertexts + privkey + candidates and return the full 7-field TallyResult (with internal decrypt/validate phase), or (b) split B10: `B10a: (dropped_voters, non_voters, validated_ballots) = decrypt_and_validate(ballots, enclave_privkey, candidates)` and `B10b: (winners, per_round_counts, eliminated_by_round) = IRV_spec(validated_ballots, candidates)`. Block E1's current step structure naturally suggests option (b).
